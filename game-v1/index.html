Build a village with every match

Coins come from active play. Village buildings multiply the coins you earn and produce materials and research resources. Complete card sets and village milestones to reach the next journey.

Four places to go

Play opens the current battle, matching board or sentence puzzle. The village goal screen has Memory, Bonus and Prestige buttons below Next journey. Sets keeps your card collection. Village contains repeatable buildings and the exchange. Tech opens building blueprints.

Memory cards

Choose a discovered word set. Turn over two cards to find a Chinese–English pair. Every board holds six pairs (twelve cards) in a 3 × 4 grid. Larger sets continue onto another board; short boards add distinct review pairs from unlocked sets. A wrong pair stays visible briefly, then turns back over. Each correct pair earns coins, plus a board-clear bonus. Reloading preserves your board and cannot award the same pair again. Completing the set lets you start a new replay.

The journey

25 core word levels follow the HSK mapping, beginning with numbers. Two small resource stages introduce the five resource names. Eight sentence stages draw ten prompts from pools of at least thirty combinations, using previously introduced words. No word stage introduces more than twenty cards. The detailed grammar reference remains in the design document; these eight playable sentence pools are the initial campaign implementation.

Battle retains the one-to-four weapon progression. Matching remains the slower break, with five pairs per board. Card rewards arrive gradually, followed by the set award. Clearing a set opens its blueprints before the village gate, so future content is never needed to buy its prerequisites. Advancement requires buildings owned, completed research and resource stockpiles. Every purchased building copy counts. Stockpiles are not spent when advancing.

Your first village

Clear the first number set for its coin reward and a one-time 250-coin settlement grant. Buy 学习 (xué xí), the study hut, for 100 coins. It produces 3 知识 (knowledge) per minute. Research a first blueprint for 2 knowledge. Build 一 for 600 coins + 8 timber + 4 stone, or choose the other path. Earn more coins in Memory or bonus play while resources accumulate.

Resources and choices

木材 timber, 石料 stone and 砖 bricks buy buildings alongside coins. Yards produce timber and stone; a kiln consumes two of each for each brick produced. The exchange buys and sells these three materials in quantities of 1 or 10. Selling returns half the current buy price, rounded down per unit. Purchases need enough coins and storage space; selling frees storage. Knowledge and insight cannot be traded. 知识 knowledge and 心得 insight are only produced by buildings and cannot be bought.

Every building can be purchased repeatedly. Each new copy costs 1.18× as much, rounded up separately for each resource. There is no land limit: buy as many copies as you can afford after meeting their unlock requirements. Copies add production and progressively smaller reward bonuses. Research unlocks once per run and follows its path's prerequisite nodes. Trade improves hits, Community improves pairs (including memory), and Study improves sentences.

Storehouses increase capacity. If a storehouse recipe exceeds your storage, use its installment button; payments are nonrefundable. Buildings cannot be removed.

Offline and prestige

Buildings produce while paused or closed, up to eight hours and storage limits. Passive coins are capped at 10% of active coins earned in the preceding day; materials and research have no such coin cap. Prestige resets coins, resources, buildings, research, active cards and lessons. It retains settings and collection history and adds +0.5 to the permanent active-coin multiplier. Sentence selection changes on the next journey.

This is an initial playable balance, not yet a validated two-to-four-month economy.

Debug

Press Space to perform a correct move or continue a reward. In Memory it completes a pair. On a village gate it only advances if the requirements are met.

Reset clears only game-v1's save, including settings and permanent progress. Concept-v4 is separate.
