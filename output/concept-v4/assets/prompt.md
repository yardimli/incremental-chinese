# Shared landscape

Generated with built-in imagegen. Used as the common game background, not as card artwork.

Use case: illustration-story. Create a reusable background painting for a premium Chinese card incremental mobile game, portrait 9:19.5. Inspired by elegant jade and parchment game interfaces: warm aged ivory rice paper across the large quiet central 70 percent, delicate watercolor mountains and mist in muted jade at the bottom quarter, a small traditional pavilion at lower right, fine bamboo leaves entering the top left and bottom left edges, tiny touches of warm gold sunlight. Rich layered atmospheric painted scenery around edges, clean pale luminous center so HTML game text and cards overlaid will remain readable. No words, no Chinese characters, no numbers, no UI, no borders, no buttons, no cards, no coins, no people. Polished hand-painted game environment, restrained texture, tasteful color, not photorealistic.
