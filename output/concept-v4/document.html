Discover. Collect. Restart.

The game opens directly on a Chinese card with an English card to tap. The dictionary behind it starts empty. No level counter or difficulty banner appears during play.

The card arena

The Chinese target slides down over the dictionary. The first pass scatters English cards around the target. From two weapons onward, equipment stays on screen between hits. Only a completed word is replaced. Three weapons introduce targets travelling toward an exit; four weapons occasionally introduce two simultaneous targets. Tap a weapon to automatically hit its matching target, without aiming. Tap the matching weapon to fire: a correct hit bursts the target and earns coins; a wrong shot shakes it and earns nothing. In the first pass, cards can be repositioned or dropped onto their pair. In later passes, drag a stationary English weapon onto a Chinese target to fire at that target; moving targets cannot be dragged. Weapons return to their row after release. Tapping still fires automatically.

With one or two weapons, four seconds of inactivity lets the target escape. With three or four weapons, targets escape when they reach the end of their path (about 8.5 or 6.5 seconds). Moving the mouse keeps the early stages awake but does not stop travelling targets. This records an unanswered turn without changing coins, revealing a new dictionary entry, or counting toward mastery. Dropping a weapon onto a target attempts that pair; dropping on empty space cancels the drag. Switching away pauses the action. For example, ignoring 三 does not master it; firing three twice makes its dictionary entry gray for the rest of that pass.

The main loop

  1. One choice. Tap the English card to earn coins and reveal the pair in the dictionary. Previously discovered entries pulse yellow.
  2. Two, three, then four choices. Each difficulty requires two correct answers per pair. Mastered pairs turn readable gray and stop appearing for that difficulty. Incorrect choices award no coins. With persistent weapons they recoil and the target remains; correct hits burst the target and immediately make room for another. Matching remains the calm break after the action.
  3. Matching. Five pairs per board. Tap two cards or drag either column onto its opposite-column pair. Match all words across two boards for a ten-word set, or three boards for a fifteen-word set. Wrong pairs can be retried without a score penalty.
  4. Gradual card reveals. The arena awards 40% of the set gradually through its four passes: four cards for a ten-card set, six for a fifteen-card set. Matching awards the remaining 60%, with small reveals between pairs. The final card is reserved for the final match. The full-screen reward page shows collection progress. The final reveal fills the set.
  5. Set award. Claim it to unlock slow passive production and move directly into the next set. Card reveals themselves do not start passive production.

Each numeric answer below 10 independently appears as digits 60% of the time and as a written word 40% of the time. For example, 7 and three can appear together. Values of 10 and above always appear as digits with comma separators. The first set has 18 cards: seven are awarded in the arena and eleven in matching.

Game 7 examples

Answers contain two to four previously introduced word or phrase cards. Ten cards are offered as choices. Tap an answer card to remove it, or drag the placed cards to change their order.

  • I drink water. → 我 / 喝 / 水
  • He also drinks tea. → 他 / 也 / 喝 / 茶
  • I have three ducks. → 我 / 有 / 三隻鴨子
  • You want hot coffee. → 你 / 要 / 熱咖啡

The thirty-sentence pool is shuffled when a new save is created. Each prestige randomly selects ten sentences from those not used in the previous run. The selection stays saved for the entire run, so reloading never changes the questions.

Coins and offline income

A new run starts with zero coins and zero automatic income. Correct taps and matches initially award 10 coins; correct sentence puzzles award 30. Each claimed set initially adds 0.10 coins/second. Active play stays much stronger than passive production.

For example, one completed set earns 6 coins per minute or 360 coins over an offline hour. Offline earnings join the same balance and can buy upgrades. There is no second currency. The timestamp is settled before mutations, so reloading cannot pay the same offline interval twice.

Boosts affect tap rewards, automatic production, offline earnings, or set payouts. Their category labels are 點擊, 自動, 離線, and 獎勵. Each purchase increases that upgrade’s multiplier by ×1.2 and raises its price.

Prestige

After collecting a set, Restart becomes available. It clears coins, cards, set progress, and upgrades; retains display settings; and raises the permanent multiplier by +0.5. Automatic income returns to zero until another set award is claimed. The sentence selection changes for the next run.

Bonus content

Sets includes a separate Bonus puzzles link. These optional four-choice quizzes use discovered content, award a small correct-answer bonus, and never advance the main set.

Files and screen components

Each screen remains a separate HTML file in screens/. Main play routes to matching, reward, and sentence screens according to saved progress; opening a future stage directly returns to the current stage.

engine.mjs owns state transitions. shared.js owns rendering, navigation, and save transactions. lessons/index.json lists the six pair files and the thirty-sentence file. All files are local; the game requires a local HTTP server to load modules and JSON.

Progress is stored in this browser under chinese-game-v1. Earlier visual-demo balances are not imported. Scores, timings and upgrade prices are initial balancing values. Tests cover the complete seven-game path, reward idempotency, mastery, wrong answers, offline accrual and prestige.
