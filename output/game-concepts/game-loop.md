# Chinese incremental game concept

## Progression

Numbers 1–10 → first 25 common words → short sentence combinations → next 25 words → new sentence combinations. Select useful high-frequency words that support simple sentences; define the exact word list during curriculum work.

Points unlock lessons. Completing quizzes activates word cards that earn points over time. Repeated correct answers improve those cards. Sentence lessons unlock multipliers for the words used in the sentence. Balance active rewards so practice remains the fastest way to advance.

## Activity rotation

- Two-column matching in either translation direction.
- Four-option meaning selection.
- Sentence tiles: arrange words into the requested translation.
- Listen and pick: select the meaning of an audio prompt.
- Missing word: complete a sentence from unlocked vocabulary.
- Tone pairs: match audio with tone-marked pinyin.
- Mini conversations: choose appropriate replies over three exchanges.

Rotate activities within lessons. Revisit mistakes sooner, with no loss of owned cards. Give a brief explanation and another attempt.

## Language display

Traditional or simplified Chinese, each with pinyin on or off; alternatively pinyin-only mode. Support Chinese-to-English and English-to-Chinese prompts. Keep script choices and learning history across prestige. Pinyin-only mode should show tonal marks and use contextual meanings where syllables are ambiguous.

## Upgrades

Introduce simple labels with audio and translations: 加倍 (jiā bèi, double) for quiz rewards ×2; 加速 (jiā sù, speed up) for card income ×1.5. Later labels use short phrases such as 一起學習 (yì qǐ xué xí, learn together). Keep English explanations available on demand.

## Prestige

Reset points, earning cards, and lesson purchases. Keep mastery history, preferences, and permanent insight. Replaying familiar lessons earns more and can use brief mastery checks instead of repeating full tutorials. Insight unlocks permanent earning bonuses and new activities. Display the exact resets, retained progress, and reward before confirmation.

All prices, rates, and multipliers in the screenshots are illustrative and require balancing. The images represent different moments of play rather than a single consecutive session.
